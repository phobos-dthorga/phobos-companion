**The world of your newest save**, drawn as a globe, in several views (biomes, climate, terrain), with pollution over any of them, and its places, roads and rivers. RimWorld only for now.

- Turn the globe with the buttons, or go **Closer** and **Further away**; **Whole planet** shows it all.
- **Find a place** by name, or pick a tile to read it.
- **Read the newest save again** after you save in the game. The first read can take a minute; after that, a few seconds. Nothing is changed.
- **On a PC that cannot draw the globe**, the page says so and lists the same facts in tables.

### Keys
- **6** opens the Atlas.
