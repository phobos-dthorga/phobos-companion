**Choices the agents made for you to revise, and questions they need your word on**, from every repository.

Each shows its options with the agent's lean marked and chosen to begin with, so **taking the lean is one press of Decide**. Pick another, or **Something else** with your own words. Answered ones wait below with **Change my answer** until an agent acts on them.

Home says how many are waiting.

### Keys
- **D** opens Decisions.
