**What the game writes, and what Steam records**, for any Steam game on your list. Nothing here is changed or deleted.

- **What changed:** whether Steam updated the game, and every Workshop item updated since you last played, the last day or the last week.
- **Saves**, newest first, autosaves marked, each with **Show in folder**.
- **Screenshots**, the game's own and Steam's, drawn small; press one to see it whole.
- **Where the space goes:** the game, its Workshop items and each folder of its data.

If a folder is not found, the page says which. Add the game's folders when [adding the game](task-add-game), or in its entry in the games list.

### Keys
- **W** opens Game files.
