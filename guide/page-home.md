**Where was I?** The page to come back to after a break. For the game you are looking at (chosen at the top of the window), it shows:

- **Your games**, each with when you last worked on it, and **Look at this** to switch to another.
- **The last thing you did**, and **the latest work**: the newest commits in its repositories. Each opens its code.
- **Left unfinished:** files changed but not yet committed.
- **Not released yet:** mods with changelog entries no released version holds; each opens its changelog on the Changes page.
- **Open work**, from each repository's list of what is still to do, where it keeps one (see [make a repository ready](task-repository-ready)).
- Counts that lead elsewhere: settings to look at, and a note when your records want a backup.
- **Checks on GitHub**, for each repository: commits not pushed, files changed, and whether its last push passed its checks on GitHub, with any nightly run that has stopped running. **Look now** asks GitHub at once; **Going online settings** switches it off (see [choose what the Companion asks the internet](task-going-online)).
- **Their code changed**, when a repository's nightly watch found commits in other authors' public source that touch a method your code names: each commit, its date and words, the method, and your line that names it. It is an early warning: read the commit before acting, and the check after an update stays the real one.
- **Safe copies**, for a repository that lists what cannot be made again: each set, whether it is copied, changed since or never copied, with **Copy now** and **Check the copies** (see [safe copies](task-safe-copies)).

**If you work with [AI agent sessions](task-agent-features)**, Home also shows who is working on what (their [claims](term-claim)), [screen notes](term-screen-note) still waiting for one, and [decisions](term-decision) waiting for you.

The very first time, with no games listed yet, Home shows [First steps](task-first-steps) instead. If **git** isn't installed, a line at the top of every page says so, with where to get it.

### Around the window
- **The pages are listed down the left**, under Play, Code and Tools, each with its key beside it. A number beside a page says how many things there wait on you: [decisions](term-decision) and picks to answer, test rows seen working that nobody has judged, and mod settings to look at. No number means nothing waits.
- **The game you are looking at is at the top**: press it to choose another, each with when you last worked on it.
- **Settings, Mods and About are in tabs**, one part at a time; a tab says when something in it needs you.

### Keys
- **1** opens Home. **[** and **]** step to the game before or after. **R** reads everything again.
