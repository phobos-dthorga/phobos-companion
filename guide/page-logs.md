**What the game's log says about its last run.** Messages that differ only in names and numbers are grouped, most serious and most frequent first, with what is new since the run before and the mods each one points at.

Open one to see its stack: a step found in your repositories opens on the [Code map](page-codemap), where its history shows the changes that touched it.

**Reporting a fault.** Under the mods a message points at:

- For a mod of **yours** on GitHub, **Report this on GitHub** opens your repository's new-issue form in your browser, filled in with the fault, how often and when, the stack and the lines as written. A report too long for the address goes on your clipboard to paste.
- For **another author's** mod whose public source is known, **Their reports** lists their issues, those naming this fault's words first, so you can see whether it is already reported. **Their new-issue form** opens their form blank: write your report yourself, after looking.

The Companion never posts anything: you read each form and press Submit there yourself.

**Explaining a fault.** Once [an AI is chosen](task-ai-services), **Explain this fault** shows what it would send and sends it only on **Send**: see [Have a fault in the log explained](task-explain-fault).

### Keys
- **G** opens Logs.
