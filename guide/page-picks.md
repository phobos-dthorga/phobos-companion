**Every picture and sound the art tools made tries of, waiting for your pick**, from every repository that keeps a list of them.

Waiting ones come first. Each shows its tries, small and side by side, and the try in focus against the one in use now, with the
same views as the code viewer: **Side by side**, **Flick between them** (**Z**), **Show what changed**, the zoom buttons and the
grounds, including the game's own (soil and dark stone for RimWorld). A sound has its players, spectrograms and the band alone.

**Compare with:** sets the try in focus against the one in use now, or against any other try, so a new picture with nothing in
use is compared too, and a correction try shows exactly what the fix touched. **Mark loose specks and fringes** paints the edge
check's findings in the accent colour, with their counts beside the picture. The zoom, the ground, the view and the marks stay as
you left them from one pick and try to the next, and the next time you open the Companion.

**Pick Try …** keeps your pick; **None of these** says none will do, with your words for what to try next. With no words
yet, it asks **What was wrong with them?** first: press a reason (**Wrong subject**, **Wrong style**, **Hard to read small**,
**Wrong colours**), type your own and press None of these again, or **Skip: none, with no words** (since 0.96.0). Cards made
for the same file show as **one pick**, marked "… cards, one file", with every try of every card; picking one answers them all.
A pick whose try is already in the game is settled by itself when the page reads, and the page says which. The next waiting pick
opens. An agent session applies it later, and it leaves the list; until then, pressing another changes it.

**Anything to add?** takes your words for the pick: what to change, what to try next, or anything the agent should know. They stay with
that pick while you look at others, and go with your pick however you make it, by button or by key. Once kept, the line at the top
says so and repeats them, and the list shows them beside the pick, read back from where they were kept.

### Keys
- **Shift + P** opens Picks.
- **J** and **K** step through the tries; **Shift + J** and **Shift + K** through the picks.
- **Ctrl + Enter** picks the try in focus; **Shift + N** says none of these.
