**Answers from the tools**, chosen from a list, with nothing to type: the same reports agent sessions run, for the game shown, and those about this PC as a whole, such as **Why did this program crash?** (see [find out what a crash or power cut did](task-after-a-crash)).

Two tabs: **Questions**, the list above, and **Look up** (**Alt + 2**), which shows one definition by its name: who defines it,
as written, or as patched (see [look up one definition by name](task-look-up-definition)).

Other reports that need something typed or chosen first, read online or change anything are not offered here yet.

- **Some reports run by themselves** and count what waits on you, such as a game's save checks on your newest save (see [find out whether my game is in a state it should never be](task-check-saves)). Their count shows beside **Reports** in the list of pages, beside the report here ("2 to look at"), and as a note on Home. Open the report to see each finding.

- **Charts** come with a report that has them, such as RimWorld's colony mood report, with **Show the numbers** for the table behind each.
- **A RimWorld save holds only half of a colonist's mood.** The mood report counts the remembered events exactly and gives the rest (rooms, pain, beliefs) as one figure, unless D'thorga Framework wrote its colony mood file at that save.

### Keys
- **G** opens Reports.
