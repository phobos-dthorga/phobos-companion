**Answers from the tools**, chosen from a list, with nothing to type: the same reports agent sessions run, for the game shown.

Reports that need something typed or chosen first, read online or change anything are not offered here yet.

- **Charts** come with a report that has them, such as RimWorld's colony mood report, with **Show the numbers** for the table behind each.
- **A RimWorld save holds only half of a colonist's mood.** The mood report counts the remembered events exactly and gives the rest (rooms, pain, beliefs) as one figure, unless D'thorga Framework wrote its colony mood file at that save.

### Keys
- **G** opens Reports.
