**What can't be made again, and whether each is copied.** Each repository lists its own work (training sets, style models,
notes) and the game's saves and settings; this page shows each set's kind and its state at every place its copies go.

- **Copy now** copies whatever changed; the copy to a repository is sent at once.
- **Check the copies** reads every copy again and says if one is missing or damaged.
- **Restore** puts a copy back into a folder you pick, after your yes; **Let go** removes a kept copy no longer needed.
- **The daily run** copies every list at the time you choose, even with the Companion closed.

Home shows a line, **Safe copies wait**, while a set has never been copied, has changed since its copy, or a copy isn't sent yet.

### Keys
- **Shift + B** opens Safe copies.
