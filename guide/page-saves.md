**What your saves hold, a part at a time.** Open any save, find anything in it by name, kind or ID, and open one thing to see everything it saved. It only reads: the save is never written.

- **Which save**: the newest unless you choose one; **Explore** beside any save on Game files opens it here.
- **The first look at a save takes about a minute**, while the tools read a frozen copy of it once. Later presses are quick until the game saves over it.
- **Find** searches names, IDs, kinds, places on the world map and the save's own parts (* and ? are wildcards). Every person, thing and part in the answer opens with a press.
- **One thing in full**: its kind as your mods label it and the mod that defines it (or **not in your mods now**, what a removed mod left behind), what holds it out to the map or the world, what it holds, and every value it saved.
- **What it holds**: the save's parts with how many of each, one level at a time; a list item shows as **item**.

### Keys
- **Shift + V** opens the Save explorer.
