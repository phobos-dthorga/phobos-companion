**How the Companion works for you, and where the tools keep and find things**, in six tabs:

- **Look and keys:** [choose or make a theme](task-change-theme), and [change any key, or turn one off](task-change-key).
- **Games:** [add a game Steam has installed](task-add-game).
- **Folders and files:** every folder and file the tools read, for every game, one line each with where its value comes from. Press a line to open it and [choose or type a new one](task-change-folder). The buttons above the list show every setting, one game's, or only those that **need you** (naming something not on this PC); the tab says how many need you.
- **Going online:** [what the Companion asks the internet, and switching each kind off](task-going-online).
- **Backups and space:** [where copies of your records go, and how fresh they are](task-records-backup), and [clean up what the tools left behind](task-clean-up).
- **Agent sessions:** [show or hide the features for AI agent sessions](task-agent-features).

### Keys
- **9** opens Settings.
- **;** and **'** step to the tab before or after; **Alt + 1** to **Alt + 9** jump to a tab by its place. **Left** and **Right** move along the tabs when one has the keyboard. Each tab is a step of the trail, so **Back** returns to the tab you left.
