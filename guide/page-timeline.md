**What happened to the game's mods over time**: everything the changelogs record, back to each repository's first commit, and every install, go-back, backup, build and texture encoding on this PC since the tools began keeping them.

- **The overview:** one row a mod and one column a day, each mark the biggest thing that happened. Press a mark to open its day. A **Screenshots** row marks the days you took pictures.
- **The days**, newest first, with every fact. **Read it as it stood** opens a changelog exactly as it was then, marking what has been reworded or taken out since.
- **What was I running then?** on any day: every mod's installed version at the end of that day.

Installs, go-backs, backups, builds and encodings are recorded from 19 Nov 2026 on, in the history log in the Companion's own folder.

### Keys
- **7** opens the Timeline.
