title: Add a game Steam has installed
kind: task
go: settings?panel=games

see: page-gamefiles, term-repository
---
1. Open **Settings** (the **9** key), on the **Games** tab, and press **Add a game from Steam**.
2. Choose the game. Games already on your list are shown, but can't be chosen twice.
3. Check its name and short name, and **where it keeps its files**: one of the guesses (each says why), **Choose a folder…**, or none for now.
4. Tick its [repositories](term-repository), if it has any yet.
5. The entry is shown exactly as it will be written. Press **Add to the list**.

The game is in the game menu at once. The list as it was is kept beside it.
