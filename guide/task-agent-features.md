**Something's off** (the **!** key), the **Decisions** page and the claims board on Home serve people who work on their mods with [AI agent sessions](term-agent-session). **They show by themselves when a repository on your list uses them**, and stay hidden otherwise.

1. Open **Settings** (the **9** key), on the **Agent sessions** tab.
2. Press **When a repository uses them** (the usual), **Always** or **Never**. It takes effect at once.

The panel says whether they are shown now, and what it found: an `AGENTS.md`, a `CLAUDE.md`, a `.claude` folder or a `config/decisions.json` in a repository, or screen notes and answers the Companion already keeps.

The Guide always keeps their entries, in a group of their own at the end.
