1. Open **Decisions** (the **D** key). Home says how many are waiting.
2. Read the question and its options. The agent's lean is marked, and chosen to begin with.
3. Press **Decide** to take the lean, or pick another option first. **Something else** takes your own words.

The next agent session acts on your answer. Until then, **Change my answer** is there.
