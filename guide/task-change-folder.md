title: Change where the tools look for something

1. Open **Settings** (the **S** key), on the **Folders and files** tab. Each setting is one line, under its game: press it to open it. **Needs you** shows only those that name something not on this PC.
2. Press **Choose a folder…** (or **Choose a file…**): Windows' own picker opens where the setting points now. What you choose is saved at once.
3. Or press **Type a path** and paste one. Explorer's **Ctrl+Shift+C** copies a folder's path; its quotes are taken off.

**Use the default** puts a setting back. A change here holds for every repository and game, unless a repository's own settings name another.
