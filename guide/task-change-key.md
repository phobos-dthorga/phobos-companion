---
title: Change or turn off a keyboard shortcut
kind: task
go: settings
words: shortcut, key, hotkey, keyboard, bind
see: page-settings
---
1. Open **Settings** (the **9** key), on the **Look and keys** tab, and press **Change keyboard shortcuts**.
2. Pick the action, then press the key you want. A key already taken is refused, and says by what.
3. Turn one off, or all of them, there too.

Every shortcut is one key, never two held together, and none acts while you type in a box. **?** lists them all.
