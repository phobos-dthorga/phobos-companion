---
title: Change or turn off a keyboard shortcut
kind: task
go: settings
words: shortcut, key, hotkey, keyboard, bind
see: page-settings
---
1. Open **Settings** (the **S** key), on the **Look and keys** tab, and press **Change keyboard shortcuts**.
2. Pick the action, then press the key you want. A key already taken is refused, and says by what.
3. Turn one off, or all of them, there too.

**1** to **9** open the pages in the bar's order: Home, Mods, Mod list, Tests, Logs, Atlas, Changes, Timeline and Code map (the owner's choice of 25 Nov 2026); **S** opens Settings and **G** Reports, **F1** the Guide, and **[** and **]** step through the games. The page list down the left shows every page's key, and **?** lists them all.

Most shortcuts are one key; where every letter was taken, a few are held with **Shift**, **Ctrl** or **Alt** (such as **Shift + W** for Publish). None acts while you type in a box.
