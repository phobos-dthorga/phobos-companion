**You don't have to do anything: the checks run by themselves** on your newest save, for a game whose repositories keep [checks](term-check). When they find something, you see it in three places:

- **A number beside Reports** (the **G** key) in the list of pages on the left.
- **A note on Home**, "The checks found something", with **See what they found**.
- **A count beside the report itself** on the Reports page, such as "2 to look at".

To read what they found:

1. Press **See what they found** on Home, or open **Reports** (**G**) and choose the report with the count beside it.
2. **Faults** come first: states the design says can never happen. Then **Worth a look**: states that may be fine. Each says where it was found (the thing and its place) and why the rule holds.
3. Look at the thing in the game. A finding is something to look at, **not a test result**: a rule can be wrong too.

### When they run
- **When the Companion starts, on Look again, and when you come back to its window** after five minutes or more away. So a save you made while playing is checked the next time you look at the Companion.
- **They only read.** Nothing in the game or the save changes. Reading a save for the first time takes a few seconds; after that it is quick.
- **They never touch the running game.** To check the running game's memory instead, use **Run the checks** on the [Explorer](page-explorer) page (it asks first, every time).

### Figures for the Tests page
Some checks also note **figures** from each save, such as a part's health, for the test list. They show on the [Tests](page-tests) page as rows **seen working**, when the figures are what the row expects, or under "not with the figures the row expects" when they aren't. **Only your verdict passes a row** (see [seen working](term-seen-working)).
