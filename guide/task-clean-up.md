1. Open **Settings** (the **S** key), on the **Backups and space** tab, at **Clean up**. It lists what the tools, builds and tests left behind, by group, with how much each holds and why it may go.
2. Choose the groups. Install backups start unchosen.
3. Press **Clean up**, and confirm.

Nothing goes that the list did not name, and nothing goes without your confirmation.
