1. Find the entry on **Changes** (the **6** key), or on **Mods**.
2. Press **Show the code**. The viewer opens on the commit that wrote it.
3. Three ways to read it: **What changed** (the lines added and removed), **The code as it now stands**, and **How it connects**.

An entry not yet committed shows every change not yet committed, new files included. A commit that wrote several entries is shown whole, and says so. In the viewer: **C**, **N** and **H** switch the views, **J** and **K** step through the files.

A changed picture, texture or sound is drawn or played instead of lines: see [Compare a changed picture or sound](task-compare-pictures-sounds).
