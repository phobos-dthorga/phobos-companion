1. **Close the game and RimSort** first: changes wait until both are closed.
2. Open **Mod list** (the **S** key) and press **Edit the list**.
3. Find the mod: type part of its name, id or author in **Find a mod**, or use **Show** and **From** to narrow the list.
4. Do what you want. **Each press is saved at once**, with a backup of the list kept first:
   - **Switch on** or **Switch off**.
   - **Move up** or **Move down** one place.
   - Type a place in the box beside it, then press **Move to**.
   - Press **Move…**, then **Put it at** on the row whose place it should take.

**Changed your mind?** Press **Undo the last change**. Press it again to go back on the change before, one at a time.

Switching a mod on puts it just before the first mod that needs it, or last. Press **What's wrong** afterwards to see whether the order still suits every mod.
