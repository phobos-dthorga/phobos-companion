1. **Close the game and RimSort** first: changes wait until both are closed.
2. Open **Mod list** (the **3** key) and press **Edit the list**.
3. **Choose the mods**: click a row, or walk the list with **Up** and **Down** and press **Space**. Choose as many as you like; **Find a mod** narrows the list.
4. Move them, any of these ways:
   - **Carry (Enter)**, then **Up**, **Down**, **Page Up**, **Page Down**, **Home** or **End**, and **Enter** to put them down (**Escape** cancels).
   - **Put after…** or **Put before…**, type part of another mod's name, and press **Enter** on the match.
   - Type a number in **Place**, then press **Move to**.
   - Drag a row with the mouse.
5. Or press **Switch off**. For mods switched off: **Switch on**, or **Switch on after…** and find the mod they go after.

**Each change is saved once, when you make it**, with a backup of the list kept first. Several mods moved together go as one block and are one change.

**Changed your mind?** Press **Undo the last change**. Press it again to go back on the change before, one at a time.

Switching a mod on without a place puts it just before the first mod that needs it, or last. Press **Sort** afterwards to see whether the order keeps every rule.
