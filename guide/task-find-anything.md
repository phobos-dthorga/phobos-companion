---
title: Find anything quickly
kind: task
words: search, find, go to, jump, lookup, slash
see: term-address, page-guide
---
1. Press **/** anywhere.
2. Type a few letters of what you want: a page, a mod's version, a changelog entry, a test row, a day, a setting, a report, a memory watch, a piece of code, or an entry of this Guide.
3. Pick it with the arrow keys and **Enter**.

You can also paste a Companion [address](term-address) there to go straight to it.
