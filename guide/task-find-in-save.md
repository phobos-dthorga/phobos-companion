1. Open the **Save explorer** (Shift + V), or press **Explore** beside the save on **Game files**.
2. Type a name, a kind or an ID in **Find** (for example a colonist's name, `Steel`, or an ID such as `Human200`) and press **Find**.
3. Press what you were looking for: its page shows **what holds it**, out to the map or the world, and **what it holds**, each a press away.

A thing whose kind says **not in your mods now** was made by a mod you no longer have: that is what a removed mod leaves in a save.
