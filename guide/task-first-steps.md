The first time the Companion opens on a PC, Home shows **First steps**. Two steps and you're ready:

1. **Where are your mod projects?** Press **Choose the folder…** and pick the folder your mod projects are kept in, each in a folder of its own. If they aren't in one folder, or you have none yet, press **They're not in one folder**.
2. **Add your first game.** Press **Add a game from Steam**, choose the game, check where it keeps its files, tick its mod projects if it has any, and press **Add to the list**.

That's all: the Companion comes with everything it needs (its own Python and [tools](term-tool-kit)), except **git**, which reads your projects' history. If git is missing, every page says so, with where to get it.

Add more games any time from **Settings**, at **Games on the list**: see [add a game](task-add-game).
