---
title: Fix a missing mod or another mod list problem
kind: task
go: modlist
words: missing mod, rimsort says missing, remove mod from list, switch on dependency, undo mod list
see: page-modlist, term-backup
---
1. **Close the game and RimSort** first: changes wait until both are closed.
2. Open **Mod list** (the **S** key). Problems are at the top of each list, each with why it happened.
3. Press the change you want, such as **Take it off the list** or **Switch it on**.
4. Read the card, then press **Yes, make the change**. A backup of the list is kept first.
5. The page checks again by itself.

**Changed your mind?** Press **Undo the last change**, then **Yes, put it back**. If the game or a mod manager saved the list since, going back is refused, so nothing they changed is lost.

**A mod you need is not on this PC?** Press **Open in Steam** and subscribe there, then press **R** to check again.
