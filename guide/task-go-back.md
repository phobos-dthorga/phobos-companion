---
title: Go back to an older version of a mod
kind: task
go: mods
words: restore, undo, rollback, revert, older version, broke
see: term-backup, task-back-up-mod, page-mods
---
1. **Close the game** first: going back waits until it is closed.
2. Open **Mods** (the **5** key) and find the mod.
3. Open its **Backups**. They are newest first, each with when it was made and its version.
4. Press **What going back changes** to see what you would gain and lose, by its changelog entries.
5. Press **Go back to this**, and confirm.

**Nothing is lost:** the backup stays, and the install it replaces becomes a new backup, so you can go forward again the same way.
