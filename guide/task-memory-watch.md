---
1. **Save your game first**: attaching to it can make it hitch for a moment.
2. Open **Memory** (the **U** key), choose how long, and press **Start a … watch…** (or the **T** key). It always asks first.
3. Play. Type what you're doing and press **Mark it** (or the **B** key), such as opening the world map or a raid starting.
4. It stops by itself, or press **Stop the watch now** (or the **X** key).

The watch says whether memory climbs steadily or in jumps, with your marks beside the figures.
