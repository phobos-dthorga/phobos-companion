1. Open **Mods** (the **5** key), at **Settings to look at**. Every setting your mods added, removed or gave a new default is listed, newest first, until you mark it.
2. **See the change** opens the code that made it.
3. Press **Looked at** when you've seen it. **Show the … looked at** lists those again.

Home says how many are new.
