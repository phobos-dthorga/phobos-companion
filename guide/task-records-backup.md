Your test verdicts, the history log, screen notes, themes, shortcuts and settings live in this PC's local app data, which your NAS does not back up.

1. Open **Settings** (the **9** key), at **Back up your records**.
2. Press **Choose the folder** and pick a folder your NAS keeps.
3. That's all: **the Companion copies them every 6 hours while it is open**, and makes up a missed copy soon after it next opens. **Back up now** makes one at once.

Old copies are never deleted. If a copy fails (a NAS asleep), Home says so and why, and it tries again within minutes.
