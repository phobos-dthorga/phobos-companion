---
title: Save a picture of a page to share
kind: task
words: screenshot, picture, share, copy, image
see: task-something-off
---
1. Press **P** (or the **Save a picture** button) on the page.
2. The picture is saved into your **Pictures** folder (in a D'thorga Companion folder) and **copied**, so you can paste it straight into a message. **Show it in its folder** finds the file.

The picture holds **the whole window**: the bar on top, the list of pages, the page with every part of it scrolled out of view, and the details beside it.

If the page is still loading, the picture waits until it has finished; **Take it now** skips the wait.
