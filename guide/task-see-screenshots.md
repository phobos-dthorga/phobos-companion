title: See your screenshots on the day you took them
kind: task
go: timeline
words: screenshots, pictures, gallery, images, photos
see: page-timeline, page-gamefiles
---
1. Open the **Timeline** (the **7** key). The **Screenshots** row marks each day you took pictures.
2. Press a mark: its day opens with its pictures, small.
3. Press a picture to see it whole, with **Show in folder**. **Back** closes it.
4. **Compare with the one before** or **after** sets it beside the screenshot taken just before or after it: flick between them
   with **Z**, or **Show what changed**. **Stop comparing** shows it alone again. **Fit**, the zoom buttons and **=** work as in the
   code viewer.

The **Game files** page (the **W** key) shows the newest screenshots too.
