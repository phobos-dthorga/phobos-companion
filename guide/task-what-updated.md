1. Open **Mods** (the **5** key). **Since you last played**, in Before you play, says what updated since the session before, and whether any update changed something our mods patch.
2. **See what changed** opens the change itself. **Keep the new builds** keeps them to compare with later.
3. **Check what they mean for ours** asks the tools what the updates mean for our mods. It takes a couple of minutes the first time, while the tools read the new builds, and changes nothing.

It answers four things:
- whether every method and field **our code** names is still there (one that is gone means our patch fails to load);
- whether an update touched the code behind one of our **temporary patches** (the ones that work around another author's fault): if so, **See what changed** opens it, to read whether the fault is fixed and our patch can come out;
- how big each update was, and the new things it brought, in case a mod now does something ours does too;
- whether everything **our XML patches** name is still there.

It says names and files still match; it does not show that anything works in play.

For every Workshop item updated, and whether Steam updated the game, open **Game files** (the **W** key): **What changed** looks back to your last launch, the last day or the last week.
