Every place in the Companion has an **address**, such as `dthorga://mods/rimworld`: a page, a game, and what is chosen on it. **Copy address** keeps one; paste it into [Go to anything](task-find-anything) to return.

The Companion keeps a **trail** of where you've been, dialogs included: **Back** (the **,** key, or the mouse's side button) and **Forward** (the **.** key) walk it.
