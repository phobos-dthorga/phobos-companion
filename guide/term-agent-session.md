An AI coding assistant (such as Claude Code) working in one of your repositories. Several may work at once; they take [claims](term-claim) so they don't collide, read your [screen notes](term-screen-note) when they start, act on your [decisions](term-decision), and can write your test verdicts into the test lists.

If you don't work with them, you need none of this: the Companion hides these features unless a repository on your list shows sessions at work. See [show or hide them](task-agent-features).
