One line of a mod's changelog: one thing that changed, under a version and a section (Added, Changed, Fixed…). Its **bold opening** names it.

The Companion compares entries to say what installing or going back would give and take away: **a changelog says what changed, not that it works in game**. The commit that first wrote an entry is how [Show the code](task-code-behind) finds its code.
