**The slow pages open with what they found last time, and read again behind it.** **Tests**, **Mods** and **Mod list** each keep their last reading. Opening one shows it at once, with a line saying when it was read and a bar for the fresh reading. **Buttons that change something wait** until the fresh reading is in, so you never act on an old one.

**Reading ahead:** while the Companion is open, it reads those pages for the game you are looking at by itself, one at a time and behind anything you ask, so they are fresh when you open them. It only reads. Switch it off in [Settings](page-settings), on the **Games** tab, under **Read the slow pages ahead**: then nothing slow runs by itself, and the number beside each page comes from that page's last reading.

**"Waiting for other readings to finish"** on a bar means three slow readings are already running. The rest take turns, so each finishes sooner than all of them at once would.

**Coming back to the window** reads **Mods** again only when something changed: the repository's last commit or its changed files, or an installed mod's folder. It cannot see the game start or close; **Read again** does.
