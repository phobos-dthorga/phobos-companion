What only you and the tools wrote, which nothing else can rebuild: your test verdicts, the history log, the settings you've looked at, screen notes, decisions, keyboard shortcuts, themes and the central settings file.

They live in this PC's local app data, which your NAS does not back up, so the Companion copies them to a folder you choose: see [keep them backed up](task-records-backup).
