A copy of something that cannot be made again, such as a training set or a style model, kept in a place you chose: a plain folder, or a private repository.

Each set has a **kind**. **Ours** may go to every place you chose. **Made from a game's files** and **Personal** (your own, but private: notes kept about you, a game's settings) go only to a folder marked private and local, never to a repository and never to a folder that looks synced or shared. A kind the Companion does not know yet is shown as **Kept private**. A training counts as ours only when every picture in it is listed by its fingerprint (its hash) in the training's own list; one picture that is not listed makes the whole training count as made from a game's files.

Safe copies are not the same as a mod's [backups](term-backup), which are copies of an installed mod.
