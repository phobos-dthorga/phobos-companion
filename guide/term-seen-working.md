**Seen working** means the game noted a feature's code running without an error, while the seen-in-play setting was on. It does **not** mean it looked right.

**Your verdict** (Passed, Failed, or partly) is the only thing that passes a test row. Until you give it, a row seen working waits on the **Tests** page for your word.
