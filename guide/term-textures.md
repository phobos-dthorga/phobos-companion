The game loads a texture's encoded copy (a `.dds` file) instead of its picture (a `.png`) whenever both are there, **whatever their dates**. So an old encoded copy shows the old picture. Encoding makes them fresh.

The Mods page says which mods have stale textures, and **Encode their textures** fixes them (with the game closed). Where you play with Waylith (a mod that changes how the game loads pictures), it also trims textures Waylith would unpack at four times the memory.
