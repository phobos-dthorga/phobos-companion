The Companion's colours, and nothing else: a name and twenty colours, one for each role (the background, words, lines, accent, and what read-from-files, worked-out, it-worked and went-wrong look like, and the charts').

Every theme is checked so its words can be read on every background. A theme file holding anything but colours is refused.
